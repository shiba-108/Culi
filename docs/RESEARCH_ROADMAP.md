# Research Roadmap

## Current Phase

Initialization complete. Research planning is pending project owner approval.

## Strategic Research Priorities (D-0006)

Future research must prioritize:

- Fundamental principles of intelligence and computation.
- New neural organization and architecture possibilities.
- Knowledge representation, memory, reasoning, and learning.
- Native model capability for tool understanding and control.
- Computational efficiency and performance.
- Experimental methods for validating intelligence under strict resource constraints.

Existing architectures remain references only.

Performance is a foundational design objective. Future research and evaluation should consider:

- Intelligence and task success per GB of RAM.
- CPU inference speed and throughput.
- End-to-end task completion time.
- Resource consumption per successfully completed task.
- Accuracy and reliability under constrained resources.

Comparisons must use fair and reproducible evaluation conditions.

## Proposed Research Phases

1. Literature review and baseline survey.
2. Hypothesis formation.
3. Hardware and memory constraint analysis.
4. Architecture exploration.
5. Data and evaluation policy definition.
6. Experiment design.
7. Controlled experimentation.
8. Result validation and conclusion recording.
